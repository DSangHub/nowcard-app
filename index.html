How Now Shopping works

Location writes the list.
The day writes the price.

01

Stand where you shop

Geolocation opens a radius of member merchants. You write the list once — milk, nails, a birthday candle — instead of opening six store apps.

02

See today’s timed rate

Each merchant posts one discount for the day: 2, 3, or 5 percent, unlocked at $25, $35, or $50. When the clock hits close, the rate expires.

03

Pay with NowCard on file

The discount is applied at the terminal or online checkout because NowCard is already tokenized onto the card you already use. No coupon code ballet.

The card, without the vault

NowCard never sees or stores sensitive data.

We tokenize. Partners authorize. Your PAN stays with Stripe and the issuing Fintec.

Link the card you have

An API lets you attach NowCard benefits to an existing everyday-use card. The day’s rate attaches in the authorization path.

Or apply for a NowCard

One click routes the application to a member Fintec. They underwrite. They issue. We never hold the application file.

Stripe + Fintec bridge

Merchant rate for the day, spend threshold, tokenized instrument, settlement. Sensitive fields terminate at PCI-scoped partners.

What we keep

Merchant membership, daily rate, threshold, token references, anonymized radius events.

What we never keep

Primary account numbers, CVV, full track data, government ID images, raw precise trails.
